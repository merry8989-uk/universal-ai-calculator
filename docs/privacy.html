Nexus Calc

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Nexus Calc is a calculator app. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does with your information. The short version: there is no account, no advertising, and no tracking. Your calculations stay on your phone.

What stays on your device

  • Everything you type into the calculator, and your calculation history.
  • Your settings — theme, language, number format, keypad layout and the rest.
  • The diagnostics log, which records app events so you can export it when reporting a problem. It never leaves your device unless you share it.
  • Cached exchange rates, so the currency converter works offline.

None of this is uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes it.

What is sent off the device

Only when you choose to use these features:

  • Ask AI. Your question, and any photo, audio clip or file you attach, is sent to the app's own backend — a Cloudflare Worker running Cloudflare Workers AI — so a model can answer. The API key is held on that server, not in the app, and you never need to enter one.
  • Voice input. Speech is recognised by your phone's own speech service, governed by your device settings.
  • Currency rates. The app fetches public exchange rates when you open the currency converter with a connection available.
  • Update checks. The app asks GitHub for the latest release version number.

Nothing is attached to your identity. There is no sign-in, no user ID and no profile, so requests cannot be tied back to you.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad SDKs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera roll or files beyond the specific item you pick to attach.

Permissions the app asks for

  • INTERNET — for Ask AI, currency rates and update checks.
  • REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES — so the in-app updater can hand a downloaded update to Android's installer. Android still asks you to confirm.

The app does not request the camera or microphone permission. When you take a photo or dictate a calculation, Android's own camera and speech apps do the work and hand the result back.

Children

The app is a general-purpose calculator and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
