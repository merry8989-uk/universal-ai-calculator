Nexus Calc

One calculator for maths, units, money, health, geometry, code and graphs.

v1.4.0
Download APK

What it does

Nexus Calc puts every calculation you need into one fast, offline-first Android app: standard and scientific maths, a 24-category unit converter, a programmer tool, date maths, statistics, matrices, numerical calculus, a live function plotter, format converters, a currency converter and 192 purpose-built calculators for money, health, geometry, physics, engineering and construction.

Features

Standard & scientific

Live preview, ans memory, trig with DEG/RAD, logs, powers, factorial, nCr/nPr and constants.

Unit converter

43 categories, 530 units — one physical quantity per category, so volts can never be converted into ohms. Fuel economy handles the reciprocal L/100 km correctly.

Graphing

Plot y = f(x) live on a canvas with an adjustable x-range.

Statistics & matrix

Full descriptive stats with regression, plus matrix add/subtract/multiply/determinant/inverse.

Calculus

Numerical derivative, second derivative, definite integral and summation of f(x).

4 dynamic themes

Cyberpunk Neon, Emerald Mint, Sunset Crimson and Midnight Gold, switchable live, with gradient keypad keys.

Ask AI

Ask anything — maths, science, money, health — and get an answer with the working. No key to configure.

279 calculators

EMI, NPV, IRR, SIP, BMI, BMR, eGFR, geometry solids, physics laws, concrete, tiles, solar sizing and more.

Every function, in detail

  • Economics calculators — GDP (expenditure), GDP growth, GDP per capita, GDP deflator, real GDP, inflation (CPI), unemployment and participation rates, the Fisher equation, money multiplier, required reserves, money creation, velocity of money, the quantity equation, price/income/cross elasticities, MPC & MPS, spending and tax multipliers, consumer and producer surplus, deadweight loss, terms of trade, balance of trade, real exchange rate, PPP, Cobb-Douglas production, labour productivity, HDI, real wage, EOQ, marginal cost & revenue, savings rate, debt-to-GDP, fiscal deficit, COLA, Okun's law and the rule of 70.
  • Ask AI tab — ask anything in plain language ("A 1200 sq ft flat at 2500 per sq ft, what is the cost?", "Is 91 prime, and why?") and get an answer with the key steps. You can also tap "Ask AI about this" on any calculation to send it as context. The assistant runs on a Cloudflare Worker, so the key lives on the server — there is nothing to configure and no key is stored on your device.
  • History — every calculation is saved on the device and kept until you clear it yourself; it survives restarts and updates.
  • Editable board — the expression is a real text field: tap anywhere to place the caret, then insert, select or delete just that part. Tap any history entry to load it back onto the board.
  • Standard tab — digits, + − × ÷, %, sign toggle (±), backspace (hold it to delete repeatedly) and clear. A ° key marks a number as an angle.
    • The result updates as you type and "=" stores the answer for reuse as ans. The percent key behaves like a normal calculator: 200 + 10% = 220, 200 - 10% = 180, 200 x 10% = 20, 50% = 0.5.
  • Scientific tab — adds sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, ln, log, sqrt, x², xʸ, π, e, factorial, 1/x, abs, nCr, nPr and round, with a DEG/RAD toggle.
  • Convert tab — pick a category and two units, type a value, and read the converted result. A swap button flips the units. 43 categories and 530 units, one physical quantity per category (so volts can never be converted into ohms). Length, Mass, Temperature, Area, Volume, Speed, Time, Data, Data rate, Pressure, Energy, Power, Force, Torque, Frequency, Fuel economy, Illuminance, Luminance, Luminous flux, Radioactivity, Absorbed dose, Equivalent dose, Current, Voltage, Resistance, Capacitance, Charge, Inductance, Conductance, Magnetic flux density, Magnetic flux, Density, Dynamic and kinematic viscosity, Cooking, Typography, Sound level, Acceleration, Volumetric flow, Mass flow, Concentration and Angle.
  • Formula reference — 1663 formulas across 13 topics, searchable by text or topic: identities, rules and tables, plus the numbered formulas read from the "1300 Math Formulas" handbook by OCR. Now also covering set theory and logic, standard limits, option pricing, stochastic calculus and interest-rate models. These are the formulas that are not calculators.
  • Formulas tab — a Tools shelf plus 279 calculators grouped into Everyday, Finance, Economics, Health, Geometry, Physics, Engineering, Construction and Math. Every input has a sensible default and results recompute as you type.
  • Statistics — paste a data set to get count, sum, mean, median, mode, min, max, range, sample & population variance and standard deviation, quartiles, IQR, skewness and kurtosis, plus linear regression (slope, intercept, R², correlation).
  • Matrix calculator — enter matrices row by row and compute A+B, A−B, A×B, transpose, determinant, trace, inverse, eigenvalues (any size), characteristic polynomial, LU, QR and least squares.
  • Distributions & tests — normal, Student-t, binomial and Poisson pdf/cdf; one- and two-sample t-tests; one-way ANOVA; chi-square goodness of fit and independence; confidence intervals.
  • Symbolic algebra — write a polynomial in x and simplify it, differentiate, integrate, evaluate, factor and find its roots — exact where the roots are rational.
  • Complex numbers — add, subtract, multiply, divide, magnitude, argument, polar form, conjugate, square and square root.
  • RPN (stack) calculator — reverse-Polish entry: push numbers, then apply operations, with the whole stack visible.
  • Calculus — type f(x) and get the derivative at a point, the second derivative, a definite integral (Simpson's rule) or a summation.
  • Graphing — three modes: plot y = f(x); a parametric curve x(t), y(t); and a 3-D surface z = f(x, y) you can turn and tilt with sliders.
  • Handwriting — write a calculation with your finger on the pad and the app reads it.
  • Voice input — tap Speak and say the calculation ("twenty percent of fifty"). It is turned into an expression on the board and sent to the assistant, which works it out and explains it. Photo solve — take a picture of some arithmetic; the text read from the photo goes to the assistant the same way.
  • x and ⇄ keys — insert the variable x (a small field appears to set its value, and the expression is evaluated with it) and swap the two sides of the loosest operator with ⇄.
  • Number format, decimal places and measuring units — results can read 30,00,000.5 (Indian), 3,000,000.5 (International) or 3.000.000,5 (European), rounded to the decimals you choose, and converters open on metric or imperial.
  • Copy, select and share — every assistant answer can be selected by hand and has Copy and Share buttons.
  • Screen-reader support — every key, tab and control has a spoken label (the glyphs like ⌫, ±, x² and √ are read as words), results are announced as they change, and a test walks the keypad so a new key cannot ship without a label.
  • Home-screen widget — add the standard calculator to your home screen, in a solid, dimmed or fully transparent style. The keys work right there, without opening the app.
  • Ask AI sits in the middle of the bottom bar as a bigger, glowing button, with two tabs on each side and Settings as a gear at the end. Every tab is outlined, pressing the tab you are already on takes it back to its own home, and the phone's back button now leaves an inner screen first and needs a second press to exit.
  • Clear buttons — a cross in the question box empties it, and every answer has Copy, Share and Clear.
  • Photo and audio reading now works — the reader is asked up to three times and the most common answer wins, and a reading that could not be confirmed is flagged so you can check it.
  • Attach to Ask AI — take a photo, or attach an image, an audio clip or a .txt/.md/.csv file. Images are read, audio is transcribed and files are opened, then the assistant solves and explains it.
  • Converters — Roman numerals ↔ numbers, number → words (Indian and international grouping), text ↔ binary / hex / ASCII, RGB ↔ hex, and timestamp ↔ date.
  • Date calculator — difference in days/weeks/months/years, working days, age, day-of-week, day-of-year, ISO week, quarter, days in month, leap year, add/subtract days and Unix timestamps.
  • Programmer tool — convert between base 2/8/10/16/36 and apply AND, OR, XOR, NOT, shifts and popcount to two numbers.
  • Currency converter — live exchange rates for the world's major currencies, converted from any base amount.
  • Settings — theme (light/dark/system), six accent colours, text size, layout density, language, notifications, live result preview, degrees by default, thousands separators, calculation history, haptic feedback on every key, fractions in results (0.75 shown as 3/4), keypad layout (Modern rounded / Classic flat), keep screen on, save state on exit, an AI assistant on/off switch, and reset to defaults.
  • Feedback — Rate app, Report issue (opens the issue tracker) and Email support, right from Settings.
  • In-app updates — "Check for updates" reads the latest GitHub Release and installs a newer signed APK directly, with no browser.
  • Diagnostics & live logs — log collection is on by default and every line is shown in a real-time log box in Settings, so you can see exactly what is being recorded. Logs stay on your device only. Share or export them as .txt, .json, .md or .zip, and clear them any time.

Settings

  • Colour theme — Cyberpunk Neon, Emerald Mint, Sunset Crimson, Midnight Gold
  • Light / dark / system mode
  • Text size and layout density
  • Language
  • Notifications
  • Live result preview, degrees default, thousands separators, history, haptics

  • Diagnostics with share / export / clear
  • Reset to defaults
  • About and version, with in-app update check

Install

Download the latest signed APK from the Releases page and open it on your Android device (Android 8.0 or newer). New versions install straight over the old one — no uninstall needed.
