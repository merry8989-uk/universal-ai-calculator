What it does

Universal AI Calculator puts every calculation you need into one fast, offline-first Android app: a standard and scientific calculator, a full unit converter, a programmer tool, a date calculator and 47 purpose-built calculators for money, health, geometry and physics — with a rich, fully-wired Settings screen and in-app updates.

Features

Standard & scientific

Live preview, ans memory, trig with DEG/RAD, logs, powers, factorial, nCr/nPr and constants.

Unit converter

12 categories, 120+ units — length, mass, temperature, area, volume, speed, data, energy and more.

Programmer tool

Binary / octal / decimal / hex conversion plus AND, OR, XOR, NOT and shifts.

Date calculator

Days, weeks, months, years, working days and full age between two dates.

47 calculators

EMI, SIP, compound interest, CAGR, ROI, BMI, BMR, TDEE, GST, tip, geometry solids, physics laws and more.

Updates & diagnostics

In-app update install and log collection with share/export/clear.

Every function, in detail

  • Standard tab — digits, + − × ÷, %, parentheses-free entry, sign toggle (±), backspace and clear. The result updates as you type and "=" stores the answer for reuse as ans.
  • Scientific tab — adds sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, ln, log, sqrt, x², xʸ, π, e, factorial, 1/x, abs, nCr, nPr and round, with a DEG/RAD toggle.
  • Convert tab — pick a category and two units, type a value, and read the converted result. A swap button flips the units. Categories: Length, Mass, Temperature, Area, Volume, Speed, Time, Data, Pressure, Energy, Power, Angle.
  • Formulas tab — choose a category and open any calculator; every input has a sensible default and the results recompute instantly as you type. 47 calculators across Everyday, Finance, Health, Geometry, Physics and Math.
  • Programmer tool — type a value in any base (2/8/10/16) and see all four at once; then apply AND, OR, XOR, NOT, or left/right shift to two numbers.
  • Date calculator — enter two dates (yyyy-mm-dd) to get the difference in days, weeks, months and years, the working days excluding weekends, and the exact age in years, months and days.
  • Settings — theme (light/dark/system), six accent colours, text size, layout density, language, notifications, live preview, degrees default, thousands separators, calculation history, haptics, an optional AI backend URL, reset to defaults, and the About/version area.
  • In-app updates — "Check for updates" reads the latest GitHub Release and installs a newer signed APK directly, with no browser.
  • Diagnostics — log collection is on by default; you can share logs or export them as .txt, .json, .md or .zip, and clear them at any time.
